Capzy: Upload and AI Processing

An AI captioning app for iOS and Android. I designed the path from tapping Create to the moment the captions are ready, so people know what is happening while the AI works.

RoleProduct Designer
CompanyUnitedreams Inc.
Timeline2024 – Present
PlatformsiOS & Android

My part: I designed the home and account screens, and the whole upload and AI-processing flow, from scratch, for iOS and Android.

Note: I rebuilt these screens with sample videos to share them here. The flows and decisions are real.

The short version

People upload a video, and AI writes the captions. A short clip takes about 10 seconds. A big file can take a minute or more. That is a long time to look at one screen. My job was to make the wait clear: what the app is doing, when the AI starts, and what to do while it works.

The flow

Six steps, from the home screen to finished captions. The wait has three parts: preparing, uploading and AI processing.

Step 1

Start a project

Create New Project is the biggest thing on the screen. Recent projects sit right below it.

Step 2

Check the language

The app detects the video’s main language. People confirm it, or change it, then upload.

Step 3

Preparing video

A grey orb moves while the app gets the file ready.

Step 4

Uploading

The same grey orb, now with a progress bar and a percentage.

Step 5

Processing with AI

The orb turns purple. This is when the AI writes the captions.

Step 6

Captions ready

My flow ends here. The captions open in the editor, ready to change.

Product decisions

Shipped · Trust

Detect the language, then ask

Captions depend on the video’s main language. If the language is wrong, every caption is wrong.

What I did: the app guesses the language and shows it before upload. People can confirm it or change it in one tap.

Why: the AI guesses, but the person decides. A wrong guess gets caught before any AI work starts.

Shipped · Feedback

Grey, then purple

The wait has three parts, and each one can take a while. I didn’t want three different loading screens.

What I did: one orb for the whole wait. It stays grey while the app prepares and uploads the file. It turns purple when the AI starts.

Why: one shape feels like one journey. The color change tells people the next stage started, even if they don’t read the text.

Designed · Not shipped yet

Notify me, then close the app

Today the processing screen asks people to keep the app open. That works for a 10-second clip. For a big file, it means a minute or more of looking at one screen.

What I designed: people can close the app while the AI works. The app sends a notification when the captions are ready.

How I’ll measure it: how many uploads reach the editor, how many people leave during processing, and how many come back from the notification.
